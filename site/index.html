What actually breaks at ten thousand connections

The happy path is the easy part. Three things decide whether a system survives contact with production.

One bad frame

A device sends something malformed in the middle of an aggregated batch. If that kills the listener, every other device goes dark with it. Bad events get isolated per event, counted, and left behind — the pipeline keeps moving.

Two writes at once

Two guests book the same property in the same instant. Correct-looking code double-books under concurrency. Fixing it took the transaction path from over two minutes to under two seconds across 15,000 listings, and the double-bookings stopped.

The restore nobody tested

A backup that has never been restored is not a backup. I have restored MongoDB replica sets and PostgreSQL into fresh servers, and migrated a live production stack from Azure to AWS in phases, holding each cutover to about five minutes.
